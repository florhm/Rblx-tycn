Key Systems & Features

🏛️

6 Mirrored Arena Plots

Arranged in two opposing rows facing a central cobblestone plaza. Plots feature unique neon accent colors and direction-aware conveyor velocity calculation.

💎

10-Tier Ore Progression

Ground floor droppers range from Dirt to Diamond, while Floor 2 unlocks Ruby Rigs, Emerald Crushers, Uranium Refineries, and Voidstone Extractors.

🏦

Cash Collection Vault

Ore earnings deposit into a plot vault instead of instant balance updates. Players step on their neon Cash Hub with HD audio and particle feedback to claim funds.

⚡

Security Laser Gate

Purchasable perimeter walls feature a front door laser grid. Non-owners touching the beam are instantly eliminated with particle effects and kill sounds.

⚔️

Armory & PvP Cash Drops

Unlock the Miner's Sword, Laser Blaster, and Rail Gun. Defeated players drop 10% of their cash as a physical gold reward for their attacker.

🌋

Volcanic Environment

Surrounded by 28 mountain peaks, 10 mesas, street lamp posts, radial paths, and low-poly trees and rocks built entirely with clean Luau procedural math.

System Architecture

The project follows clean object-oriented patterns in Luau, modular configuration, and Rojo directory mapping:

Rblx-tycn/ ├── default.project.json # Rojo project mapping ├── README.md # GitHub repository documentation ├── docs/ # GitHub Pages site │ └── index.html # Interactive showcase page └── src/ ├── ReplicatedStorage/ │ └── TycoonConfig.luau # Centralized layout, pricing, droppers & weapons config ├── ServerScriptService/ │ ├── Main.server.luau # Leaderstats, plot manager, PvP cash drop loop │ ├── PlotBuilder.luau # Procedural geometry generator for plots & weapons │ ├── Tycoon.luau # OOP Tycoon instance manager, sales, lasers & weapons │ └── WorldBuilder.luau # Volcanic landscape, plaza, paths & mountain horizon └── StarterPlayerScripts/ └── HudClient.client.luau # Local player HUD hint overlays
